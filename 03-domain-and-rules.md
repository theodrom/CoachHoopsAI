# Domain and Rules

## Domain Concepts

- TeamStats
- ProblemTag
- Level
- Suggestion
- SuggestionCategory

## Rules Engine

StatRulesEngine evaluates:
- team stats
- opponent stats
- RulesProfile thresholds

Rules are:
- deterministic
- flat (no nesting)
- explainable

## Philosophy

Rules represent “coach-agreeable” heuristics, not absolute truth.
They are signals for AI prompting, not final judgments.
